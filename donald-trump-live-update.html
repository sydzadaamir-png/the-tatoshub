Donald Trump Live Update: “If Americans Tire of Me, I’ll Go to Israel and Become Prime Minister”

The xxl · Nov 5, 2026

US President Donald Trump said in a Fox News phone interview on Election Day, Tuesday, November 3, 2026, that if Americans ever got tired of him, he would move to Israel and become prime minister — claiming his approval rating there stands at 99 percent.

“In fact, if people ever got tired of me in the U.S., I’ll move to Israel and become prime minister,” Trump said during the interview, as voters across the country headed to the polls for the midterm elections.

What else he said

In the same appearance, Trump said he and Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu had made a deal to stop $4 billion in annual US military aid to Israel. Democrats swept the House of Representatives and the Senate in the midterm elections held the same day.

Not the first time

It is not the first time Trump has made the joke. In May 2026, speaking to reporters before a Coast Guard graduation, he made almost the same claim about his popularity in Israel. In practice, becoming Israel’s prime minister would require membership of the Knesset, the country’s parliament, and Israeli citizenship.

Sources: The National, UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News, Baller Alert and The Blast, reporting Trump’s Fox News interview of Nov 3, 2026. The Capital TV graphic circulating on Facebook carries the same quote in Urdu.
