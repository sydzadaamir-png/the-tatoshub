How to Upload Bulk Reels on Facebook

The xxl · A practical guide for page owners

Uploading reels one by one is slow. If you run a page that posts every day, you need a bulk workflow: prepare many reels at once, then schedule them and let Facebook publish on time. Facebook has no single button that uploads 20 reels in one click — but this workflow gets you the same result.

1. Prepare in batches

Record and edit 5 to 10 reels in one sitting instead of one at a time. Keep every video vertical (9:16), 15 to 90 seconds long, and write your captions and hashtags in a notes file so posting is just copy and paste.

2. Schedule with Meta Business Suite (free)

Business Suite is Facebook's own free tool, and it is the most reliable way to queue reels:

  1. Open business.facebook.com on a computer and pick your page.
  2. Go to Content, then click Create reel.
  3. Upload your video, add a caption, hashtags and a thumbnail.
  4. Choose Schedule, pick the date and time, and click Schedule.
  5. Repeat for each reel — you can queue a full week of posts in one sitting.

Your reels then go live automatically at the times you set, even while you are offline.

3. Use a bulk scheduling tool

If you want a calendar view, tools like Buffer, Hootsuite and Later connect to your page and let you upload several videos at once, drag them onto a calendar and set posting times. Most of them are paid, and free plans are limited — but for a busy page they save hours every week.

4. Upload from the Facebook app

For quick posts, open the Facebook app, tap +, choose Reel, pick a video and tap Share. This is one reel at a time, so it is best for same-day posts, not for bulk work.

Tips that actually help reach

  • Post consistently, 1 to 3 reels a day, at the times your audience is online.
  • Hook viewers in the first 2 seconds — most people scroll past fast.
  • Add captions: most people watch reels with the sound off.
  • Use trending audio, and reply to comments in the first hour.
The fastest honest way to post reels in bulk: prepare in batches, schedule everything in Business Suite, and let Facebook publish while you work on the next batch.
